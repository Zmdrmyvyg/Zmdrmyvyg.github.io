I am an M.S.E. student in Robotics at the University of Pennsylvania, where I am working with [Prof. Nadia Figueroa](https://nbfigueroa.github.io/) in the [Figueroa Robotics Lab](https://figueroa.seas.upenn.edu/), part of the GRASP Laboratory.

My interests lie at the intersection of **robot learning, imitation learning, and motion planning**. I am particularly interested in making learned robot policies reliable and adaptable when they encounter previously unseen constraints.

Prior to Penn, I received my B.E. in Artificial Intelligence from Tongji University.
