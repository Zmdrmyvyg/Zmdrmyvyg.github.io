I am developing a replanning framework that enables frozen generative imitation policies to satisfy previously unseen geometric, smoothness, and dynamics constraints without retraining or constraint-specific demonstrations.

Our approach learns action-conditioned bilinear latent dynamics and performs receding-horizon trajectory optimization to find feasible actions while preserving the nominal policy's predicted task evolution.
