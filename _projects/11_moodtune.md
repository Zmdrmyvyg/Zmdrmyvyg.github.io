Developed a multimodal emotion-recognition model combining audio embeddings with user-interaction signals, and built a personalized music recommendation pipeline that mapped inferred affective states to therapeutic music selections.

The work was presented at **APIT 2025** in Hong Kong and received the **Best Presentation Award**.
