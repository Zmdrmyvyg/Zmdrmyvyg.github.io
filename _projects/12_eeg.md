Developed an EEG-Conformer and cross-subject evaluation pipeline for fatigue decoding on a curated EEG–KSS dataset of more than 300 participants. I also optimized the CUDA-based PyTorch training workflow and implemented tests for reproducibility.

This work contributed to a patent application.
