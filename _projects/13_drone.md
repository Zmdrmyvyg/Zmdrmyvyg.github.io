Built a ROS 2 perception–control pipeline for autonomous drone landing using YOLO, PyTorch, and OpenCV. I integrated vision-based pose estimates into a closed-loop controller, optimized asynchronous communication for low-latency updates, and validated the system in Gazebo and real-flight experiments.
